# Use cases

CaseGuard AI reviews technical support cases: it reads the written record of a case, works out what was known at every decision, asks whether a safer or cheaper step was available then, simulates the alternative from that moment only, scores the case, and turns reviewed good paths into governed, reusable guidance (SkillDNA). This page says **who does what with it**: the five personas the product was designed for, the concrete tasks each can do today, and the routes and pages behind every task.

**Scope and honesty.**

- Everything here is implemented, on **synthetic data only**; the routes are exercised by the API tests and the pages by the web component tests (jsdom, not a real browser unless the end-to-end suite was run). The AI provider is the deterministic offline engine; no language model is called ([AI_ORCHESTRATION.md](AI_ORCHESTRATION.md)).
- Every score, range and simulated outcome is an **estimate** from authored priors and is labelled as one ([RESPONSIBLE_AI.md](RESPONSIBLE_AI.md#5-communicating-uncertainty)).
- Route ids (for example `analysis.start`) are the ids of the route registry; paths, permissions and shapes are in the generated tables of [API.md](API.md#route-reference). Page names are the 12 navigation destinations of the web app (`apps/web/src/lib/nav.ts`).
- "Limits" lists the entries of [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) that matter for the persona. The roles and their permissions are in the matrix of [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#4-rbac-matrix).

## The personas

The five personas of the product specification map to the five roles of the code. In a local run each has a seeded development login; Fabi is a second-tenant engineer that exists only to demonstrate tenant isolation.

| Persona                             | Role in the code | Development login     | Permissions | Mostly works in                                                             |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------- | ----------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Technical support engineer          | `engineer`       | `user_ava_engineer`   |          10 | Cases, Case Timeline, Evidence Ledger, Hypothesis Arena, Counterfactual Lab |
| Support quality reviewer            | `reviewer`       | `user_riley_reviewer` |          12 | Evidence Ledger, Counterfactual Lab, Quality Review, SkillDNA Library       |
| Support manager                     | `manager`        | `user_morgan_manager` |           5 | Command Center, Quality Review, Efficiency Monitor                          |
| Knowledge or skill curator          | `skill_curator`  | `user_casey_curator`  |          11 | SkillDNA Library                                                            |
| Administrator and security reviewer | `administrator`  | `user_alex_admin`     |          30 | Governance, System Health, Demo Guide                                       |

The pages are shared: every persona can open a page their role may read, and an action the role may not take is refused by the API with a 403, whatever the page shows.

## 1. Technical support engineer

The engineer brings a case in, looks at what was known when, and acts on what the analysis shows. Development login `user_ava_engineer`.

| Task                                     | Page                                        | Routes (permission)                                                                                                                                              | What happens today                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bring a case in                          | Cases, New case                             | `samples.list`, `examples.list`, `examples.get`, `ingestion.preview`, `ingestion.previewFile`, `cases.create`, `cases.createFromFile` (`case:ingest`)            | A three-step wizard (source, review, create). Sources: a built-in sample, pasted text or CSV, a JSON bundle, a file. The review step shows the redaction and prompt-injection findings and the content hash before anything is stored; the case is stored as an immutable record, and the same content again stores nothing new. |
| Run or re-run the analysis               | Case overview                               | `analysis.start` (`case:analyze`, 202 and a job), `analysis.latest`, `jobs.get`, `jobs.list`, `jobs.cancel`                                                      | Starts a background job with named stages and polls its progress. Identical input never re-runs unless forced; the panel shows the provider, model, knowledge pack, configuration and input hash of the run.                                                                                                                     |
| See what was known when                  | Case Timeline, Case overview (twin)         | `timeline.get`, `twin.get`, `events.get`, all with `asOf` (`case:read`)                                                                                          | The timeline with delays and repeated diagnostics, and the digital twin as of a chosen instant. A view as of an earlier instant is **recomputed from the events recorded by then**; later events and later reviewer decisions are withheld ([API.md](API.md#temporal-reads-asof)).                                               |
| Inspect the evidence                     | Evidence Ledger                             | `evidence.list` (`case:read`)                                                                                                                                    | Claims classified into eight categories, filterable by category, review state and hypothesis, with the critical evidence that is still missing. The original machine classification is kept when a reviewer corrects it.                                                                                                         |
| Compare hypotheses and choose a step     | Hypothesis Arena                            | `hypotheses.list`, `actions.list`, `actionValue.rank` (`case:read`)                                                                                              | Competing hypotheses with confidence ranges over time, and the candidate next actions ranked with the calculation and its sensitivity shown. Confidence is an estimate from an authored knowledge pack, never a statistical truth.                                                                                               |
| Act on live quality warnings             | Quality Review (per case)                   | `warnings.list` (`case:read`), `warnings.dismiss` (`warning:dismiss`)                                                                                            | Warnings replayed event by event as of the time cursor; dismissing one needs a reason and the current version, and is audited.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Explore a better path at a decision fork | Counterfactual Lab                          | `forks.list`, `forks.get`, `graph.get` (`case:read`), `counterfactuals.run` (`counterfactual:run`, 202 and a job), `counterfactuals.list`, `counterfactuals.get` | Decision forks as they looked at decision time. A counterfactual run simulates alternative strategies from that moment only, with a recorded leak check against the final resolution; everything is labelled simulated and estimated.                                                                                            |
| Reuse a validated skill                  | Quality Review (per case), SkillDNA Library | `skillMatches.list` (`skill:read`, with `asOf`), `skillMatches.apply`, `skills.apply` (`skill:apply`)                                                            | Shows which `pilot`, `validated` or `published` skills match the case on the evidence known at the chosen instant, and records that the engineer adopted the guided plan, with its conformance. An applied match survives the skill being retired, flagged.                                                                      |
| Propose a skill from a good path         | Counterfactual Lab                          | `skills.extract` (`skill:extract`, 202 and a job)                                                                                                                | Distils a draft skill from a counterfactual path a reviewer accepted; it starts as a draft and can only reach `published` through the governed lifecycle (section 4).                                                                                                                                                            |

**Limits that matter:** only the `teams-authentication` workload can be analysed (PL-1); a time-travel view has no Avoidable Support Effort figure (PL-3); an analysis runs inside the API process and a very large case can delay other requests (OP-1, OP-3).

## 2. Support quality reviewer

The reviewer checks the analysis rather than doing the investigation: it corrects classifications, decides on simulated paths and reads the quality assessment. Development login `user_riley_reviewer`.

| Task                                         | Page                      | Routes (permission)                                                                         | What happens today                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Correct an evidence classification           | Evidence Ledger           | `claims.review` (`evidence:review`)                                                         | Records a category, a reason and the expected version. The original AI classification is preserved, the change is audited, and the correction is invisible in any earlier as-of window.                                                                                  |
| Accept or reject a simulated path            | Counterfactual Lab        | `counterfactuals.review` (`counterfactual:review`)                                          | A decision with a reason. Only an accepted path can seed a skill extraction.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Check a path against hindsight               | Counterfactual Lab        | `counterfactuals.hindsight` (`quality:review`)                                              | A post-hoc comparison that uses the final resolution and never feeds generation; the result is cached for 60 seconds.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Override an action ranking                   | Hypothesis Arena          | `actionValue.override` (`actionvalue:override`)                                             | Records the reviewer's rank for an action as of an instant, with a reason. The latest decision wins (PL-5). The ranking lists the overrides it may show (`reviewerOverrides`); an earlier window shows only those decided by then, never how many came later.            |
| Read the quality assessment and the coaching | Quality Review (per case) | `quality.get`, `metrics.get`, `coaching.get`, `warnings.list` (`quality:read`, `case:read`) | The Case Quality Index with its dimensions, penalties and range, the metric estimates, and a constructive coaching report. Coaching describes the route taken, never the person, and nothing ranks people ([RESPONSIBLE_AI.md](RESPONSIBLE_AI.md#4-non-blaming-design)). |
| Validate a skill                             | SkillDNA Library          | `skills.simulationTest` (`skill:validate`), `skills.transition` (`skill:review`)            | Replays the skill against its source case at the original decision point, runs the regression suite, and records the validation with its scope; only a full run can move a gate. Reviewers cannot approve or publish.                                                    |
| Read the audit trail and recorded decisions  | Governance                | `governance.audit`, `governance.reviews` (`audit:read`)                                     | The append-only, hash-chained audit log and the recorded reviewer decisions, paginated.                                                                                                                                                                                  |

**Limits that matter:** decision forks are read-only, so there is no fork review action even though the permission exists (PL-6); skill validation on cases other than the source rests on inferred expectations (PL-7); values are estimates with no human-labelled ground truth (VG-9).

## 3. Support manager

The manager follows portfolio-level quality and effort, with privacy-safe figures. Development login `user_morgan_manager`.

| Task                               | Page                       | Routes (permission)                                               | What happens today                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| See quality at a glance            | Command Center             | `dashboard.commandCenter` (`case:read`)                           | An aggregate for the case asked for, else the most recently analysed one, with notices when the case is missing or has not been analysed.                                                     |
| Follow quality and effort trends   | Quality Review (portfolio) | `analytics.overview` (`analytics:read`)                           | Privacy-safe analytics: quality, avoidable-effort patterns, SkillDNA adoption and effectiveness. It never ranks individuals, and effectiveness for synthetic cases demonstrates the pipeline. |
| Compare two cases                  | Quality Review             | `compare.cases` (`analytics:read`)                                | Exactly two different cases of the tenant, using their stored metric estimates; everything is an estimate and says so.                                                                        |
| Watch token and compute efficiency | Efficiency Monitor         | `efficiency.overview`, `efficiency.executions` (`telemetry:read`) | Real versus simulated path tokens and model executions, with measured, estimated and simulated values labelled as such. With the deterministic provider every token count is simulated.       |
| Browse the skill library           | SkillDNA Library           | `skills.list`, `skills.get`, `skills.versions` (`skill:read`)     | Read-only: lifecycle, versions and effectiveness. A manager cannot change a skill.                                                                                                            |

**Limits that matter:** a manager holds `case:read` but not the review or analysis permissions, and cannot dismiss warnings; portfolio figures come from two synthetic cases (VG-9, PL-2); efficiency figures are simulated with the default provider (VG-1).

## 4. Knowledge or skill curator

The curator owns the skill lifecycle: draft, validation, approval, publication, refinement and retirement, with four-eyes approval at the stages that need it. Development login `user_casey_curator`.

| Task                                   | Page             | Routes (permission)                                                                                       | What happens today                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Review drafts and versions             | SkillDNA Library | `skills.list`, `skills.get`, `skills.versions`, `skills.diff` (`skill:read`)                              | The library, a skill's allowed transitions, its version history and a human-readable diff between two versions.                                                                                                                                             |
| Move a skill through its lifecycle     | SkillDNA Library | `skills.transition` (`skill:review` at the route; the gate of the target state checks its own permission) | Nine states from `discovered` to `retired`, a reason of at least five characters and an optimistic version on every step; the pilot approver and the publisher must differ from the creator and from each other, and **publication is always a human act**. |
| Propose a refinement from another case | SkillDNA Library | `skills.proposeMutation` (`skill:mutate`, 202 and a job), `skills.versionDecision` (`skill:approve`)      | A new version learned from another case. It stays a proposal that a different person must approve, with validations on the new content; it is never applied automatically.                                                                                  |
| Validate and approve                   | SkillDNA Library | `skills.simulationTest` (`skill:validate`), `skills.transition`                                           | Only a full validation of the version's current content can satisfy or fail a gate; a run on one case is stored but inconclusive.                                                                                                                           |
| Roll back                              | SkillDNA Library | `skills.rollback` (`skill:rollback`)                                                                      | Creates a new version with the content of an earlier one the current version derives from; a version that an earlier rollback withdrew cannot be restored.                                                                                                  |
| Withdraw a skill                       | SkillDNA Library | `skills.transition` (`skill:retire`)                                                                      | Deprecating a published skill or retiring any skill needs no second person: withdrawing only reduces what a skill can do, so it is recorded as a rejection, which the creator may make. Stored applications of the skill are kept and flagged.              |
| Watch usage and effectiveness          | SkillDNA Library | `skills.get`, `skillMatches.list` (`skill:read`)                                                          | Usage and an estimated effectiveness from the 10 most recent applications. For synthetic cases that demonstrates the measurement pipeline, not a real-world gain.                                                                                           |

**Limits that matter:** regression expectations on non-source cases are inferred (PL-7); effectiveness is an estimate over few applications (PL-7); the whole lifecycle has run only against the repository and the deterministic engine (VG-9).

## 5. Administrator and security reviewer

The administrator governs configuration, roles, audit and data, and watches the health of the system. Development login `user_alex_admin`; the role holds all 30 permissions.

| Task                                 | Page          | Routes (permission)                                                                                 | What happens today                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Change governed settings             | Governance    | `governance.get`, `governance.prompts`, `governance.update` (`governance:read`, `governance:write`) | Settings (redaction, retention, model limits, export controls and others) are versioned, validated and audited; a stale version is a 409, and secret redaction categories cannot be switched off. Retention windows are stored but not enforced (SP-13).                                 |
| Manage roles and users               | Governance    | `governance.users`, `governance.assignRoles` (`governance:read`, `roles:manage`)                    | Role changes are audited with before and after roles, and the last active administrator cannot be removed. With Entra as the identity provider, roles come from Entra and the route answers 409 `entra_managed` (PL-8).                                                                  |
| Read and verify the audit trail      | Governance    | `governance.audit`, `governance.auditVerify` (`audit:read`)                                         | The append-only, hash-chained log, and a verification that walks the whole chain per tenant. The chain is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof (SP-12).                                                                                                                                      |
| Export data                          | Governance    | `governance.export` (`export:data`)                                                                 | Exports a case or the audit trail when the export controls allow it, refuses (never truncates) above the record cap, and audits the export before returning data. A case export holds the record, not the derived analysis (PL-11).                                                      |
| Delete a case                        | Case overview | `cases.delete` (`case:delete`)                                                                      | Erases the case and everything derived from it, including stored idempotent responses and the mirrored payload, and refuses while a job for the case is queued or running.                                                                                                               |
| Watch system health and the provider | System Health | `system.health`, `system.metrics`, `system.provider` (`telemetry:read`)                             | Dependency health, migrations, the AI provider status and in-process metrics, with an honest label when a dependency is a local stand-in. A provider that cannot be created is reported as degraded while deterministic code serves.                                                     |
| Run the guided demo                  | Demo Guide    | `demo.state` (`case:read`), `demo.seed`, `demo.reset` (`demo:manage`)                               | A 13-step story derived from stored facts. Seed and reset work only in a synthetic demo tenant, the `portfolio` profile plays the whole story as the seeded personas, and a reset keeps users, settings and the audit trail ([LOCAL_DEVELOPMENT.md](LOCAL_DEVELOPMENT.md#82-demo-data)). |

**Limits that matter:** the development login has no real authentication (SP-6); Entra and the Azure services have never been exercised for real (VG-2, VG-3, VG-4); a deployed environment gets its tenant from the migration job, which was verified on PGlite only, and creates no demo personas (OP-7); the OpenAPI document is served in more environments than "deployed" suggests (SP-9).
