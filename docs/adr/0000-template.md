# ADR NNNN: Short title of the decision

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: who made or approved the decision

Keep an ADR short (about one page) and truthful: record what was decided and why, not what was hoped. If a
part is not implemented or not validated, say so. Change the status instead of rewriting history; a new ADR
supersedes an old one.

## Context

What problem or force is behind the decision? Include the constraints that matter (requirements, risks,
assumptions such as `A-11`, threat ids such as `T3`) and link to them.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two clear paragraphs or a short list. Name the chosen option explicitly.

## Consequences

- Good: what gets easier, safer or cheaper.
- Bad: what gets harder, what is now a risk or a follow-up, and how it is mitigated.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A**: why it was not chosen.
- **Option B**: why it was not chosen.

## Notes

Links to code, tests, docs and issues; revisit triggers ("reconsider when ...").
